How to pay

Choose a plan in your account and pay with cryptocurrency via Trybit.

1. Open your account

Go to your account page and sign in with your email address.

2. Choose a plan

Open the Buy VPN section and choose a subscription period. Prices are shown in USD for convenience.

3. Pay with cryptocurrency

After choosing a plan, you will be redirected to a Trybit payment page. Available currencies and networks are shown on the payment page.

4. Wait for activation

After the payment is confirmed, your internal balance is updated and the VPN service is activated automatically.

Important.
Prices are shown in USD for convenience. The internal balance is RUB-based. The final crypto invoice is calculated from the internal billing amount by the payment provider.

Sign in to your account to choose a plan and pay with cryptocurrency.
