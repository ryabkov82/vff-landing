Set up VPN on Android

Install a compatible app, import your subscription link, and connect to a server.

Before you start.
Make sure you have an active subscription and a connection or subscription link from your account.

Install a compatible app

For Android, install a compatible app, then open your account and add your profile or subscription.

Happ — Google Play Happ — APK

These are third-party clients. If a link does not open or the app version changed, use the latest instructions in your account.

Step-by-step setup

1. Open your account

Open your account page and make sure your VPN subscription is active.

2. Buy or activate a VPN subscription

Choose a plan and pay with cryptocurrency if you do not have an active subscription yet. See How to pay for details.

3. Copy or open your subscription/connection link

Copy the subscription or profile link from your account page.

4. Install the recommended Android VPN client

Install one of the apps listed above. If you are unsure which app to use, check your account for the recommended client.

5. Import the subscription

Open the app, tap «+» or «Add subscription», and paste the copied link. Wait until the server list updates.

6. Connect to a server

Choose a server from the list and tap connect. Android will ask for permission to create a VPN connection — tap OK or Allow.

7. If it does not work

Try another server or update your profile in the app. If the VPN still does not connect, open VPN does not connect.

Need help with setup?

Contact support — we can help you choose an app and import your subscription.
