Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how data is processed when using the digital service providing access to a secure VPN connection VPN for friends (the “Service”), available at vpn-for-friends.com.

This English version is provided for convenience. If there is any discrepancy, the Russian version may be used as the primary version.

1. General provisions

The Service provides a digital subscription for access to a secure VPN connection. We process only the data necessary to provide the service, process payments, support users, and maintain security.

The Service does not claim absolute anonymity guarantees. The level of protection depends on device settings, the application used, network infrastructure, and the user’s own actions.

2. Data that may be processed

  • Telegram user ID and username when contacting us via the bot or support;
  • email address if you contact support by email, sign in to your account, or provide it during payment;
  • technical payment data: amount, currency, status, order identifier, payment method (to the extent available to the Service);
  • subscription and connection profile data required to activate and renew access;
  • technical logs: IP address when accessing the website, browser and device information, request timestamps, and service events for diagnostics and security.

3. Purposes of processing

  • providing and renewing digital access to the Service;
  • processing payments and maintaining order records;
  • technical user support;
  • notifications about subscription status and important changes to the Service;
  • security, abuse prevention, and incident investigation.

4. Payment data

Payment may be made through payment partners. Bank card details and other sensitive payment data are processed directly by payment partners in accordance with their policies. The Service receives only the information necessary to confirm payment and activate access.

5. Sharing data with third parties

Data may be shared with payment partners, hosting providers, and other contractors that help operate the Service — to the extent necessary for their functions and subject to appropriate safeguards.

We do not sell users’ personal data.

6. Storage and protection

Data is retained for as long as necessary for processing purposes, contract performance, payment accounting, and compliance with applicable requirements. Organizational and technical measures are applied to protect information from unauthorized access.

7. User rights

You may contact support to request clarification, correction, or deletion of data where this does not conflict with legal requirements or the need to retain data for payment accounting and security.

8. Contacts

For questions about data processing, contact:

  • Telegram: @friends_connect_support
  • Email: support@vpn-for-friends.com

The Service may update this Policy. The current version is published on this page.
